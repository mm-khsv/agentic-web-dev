# Procedure
1. Restate the user's goal.
2. Separate confirmed requirements from assumptions.
3. Identify conflicts, risks, and missing business decisions.
4. Ask a concise batch of high-impact questions.
5. Offer recommended defaults without treating them as approved.
6. Record the answers in a reviewable artifact.
